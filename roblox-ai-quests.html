How to generate quests with AI in Roblox

Stop shipping the same five quests. Cortex writes missions tuned to the player's level and story on the fly, so content never runs dry.

One server-side request

Put a ModuleScript in ServerStorage and call it from a server Script — your key never touches the client.

local Cortex = require(game.ServerStorage.Cortex)
local ai = Cortex.new("YOUR_KEY")

print(ai:ask(
  "You are a quest-giver. Give ONE short quest, 1 sentence.",
  "player is level 6 and loves exploring caves"
))

That's it — ~3 seconds, and you pay in Robux via a game pass. No cards, no backend.
